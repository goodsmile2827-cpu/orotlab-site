CrossGallery

Move photos and videos between iPhone and Android at full quality.
No server in between.

Coming soon · App Store · Google Play

If you use both an iPhone and an Android phone, moving a single photo is always a chore. Messengers shrink it; cloud storage wants an account and space. CrossGallery sends photos and videos directly between the two devices on the same Wi-Fi.

Original quality

Nothing is re-encoded or downscaled. Capture details come along too.

No server

No account, no sign-in. We keep nothing, and there are no ads or analytics.

Encrypted transfers

Confirm the same six-digit code on both screens once, and the devices remember each other.

Live Photos stay live

Live Photos from an iPhone move on Android, and the other way around.

Remembers what you sent

See only what you haven't sent yet, so moving hundreds of photos over days stays easy.

A real gallery

Date grid, full-screen viewer, albums, favorites, filters by type, trash, light and dark.

What you need

  • Both devices on the same Wi-Fi
  • CrossGallery installed and open on the other device
  • Guest networks or routers that block device-to-device traffic won't work
